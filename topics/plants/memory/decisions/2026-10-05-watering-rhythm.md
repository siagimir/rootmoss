# Watering rhythm

**Type**: decision
**Status**: active
**Created on**: 2026-10-05

**Decision**: Water **twice a week** (Tuesday + Saturday), checking
soil with a finger first — not on a fixed daily schedule.

## Why

- Daily watering killed a previous basil (overwatering lesson).
- Soil-touch check beats the calendar: dry top 2 cm = water, else skip.
- Ficus prefers to dry out more; it gets skipped more often.

## Revisit if

- Yellow leaves return (then re-check the schedule, not the calendar).
- Winter light changes dry the soil much slower.
