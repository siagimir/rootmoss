# The yellow leaves were the light, not the watering

**Type**: lesson
**Status**: active
**Created on**: 2026-10-22

- October blamed the watering, and the finger-check habit that followed
  was built on that diagnosis. Two weeks of careful watering and the
  basil yellowed again anyway.
- What actually turned it: the north-facing kitchen. Same symptom,
  different cause. A plant with no light cannot use the water it has.
- Moved to the south window, watering rhythm unchanged, recovered in
  about two weeks.
- Worth keeping the earlier note. It was a reasonable call with what
  was visible then, and seeing what I believed last month is how the
  pattern showed up.
