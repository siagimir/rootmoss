# Yellow leaves meant overwatering, not underwatering

**Type**: lesson
**Status**: superseded
**Superseded by**: `2026-10-22-yellow-leaves-was-the-light.md`
**Created on**: 2026-10-12

- Yellow + soft leaves on the basil = **too much water**, not too
  little; the instinct to water more was wrong.
- Fix that worked: stopped daily watering, moved to the finger-check
  rhythm (see `../decisions/2026-10-05-watering-rhythm.md`).
- Generalized: check the soil, don't water on feelings.
